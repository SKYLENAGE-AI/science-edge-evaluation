01 / WHY SEE

A benchmark for the work between the lines.

Laboratory science is not a textbook exercise. It means interpreting imperfect observations, connecting methods across disciplines, and knowing where the evidence ends.

01

Grounded in practice

Questions come from peer-reviewed literature, experimental figures, and first-hand research scenarios—not synthetic textbook prompts.

REAL LABORATORY DATA
02

Bounded by evidence

Every answer must be licensed by the observations provided. SEE probes whether models recognize when the available evidence is insufficient.

EVIDENCE-BOUNDED REASONING
03

Connected across fields

Chemistry, biology, and materials science intersect in the same question, testing coordination rather than isolated factual recall.
